Privacy policy

Rihla uploader is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • What it accesses: only the owner's own YouTube channels, after the owner signs in. Permissions: upload videos and read channel info.
  • What it stores: one sign-in token, kept only on the owner's computer. Nothing else is collected, sold or shared.
  • Rules it follows: it uses YouTube API Services and follows the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy and the Google API Services User Data Policy.
  • Removing access: revoke it any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: open an issue on this site's GitHub repo.
